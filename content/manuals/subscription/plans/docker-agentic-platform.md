> [!TIP]
> Docker Agentic Platform signups receive a one-time promotional
> credit toward cloud compute usage. To review your balance, see
> [Credits](/manuals/billing/_index.md#credits).

Docker Agentic Platform brings
[MCP Gateway](/manuals/ai/mcp-catalog-and-toolkit/mcp-gateway.md) and
[Cloud Sandboxes](/manuals/ai/sandboxes/_index.md) onto the Agentic Platform UI. From the Agentic Platform UI, developers can orchestrate their agents in isolated compute environments, manage MCP Gateway access with higher tool-call
allowances, and interface with Gordon to understand how everything fits together.

Docker Agentic Platform is a pay-as-you-go plan. There is no
recurring subscription fee, so you pay for the usage you accrue. To add it to
your Docker subscription, see
[Manage plans](/manuals/subscription/manage.md).

## Usage

After you subscribe, your invoice reflects metered, pay-as-you-go usage.
Docker meters compute usage based on the vCPU and memory that your sandboxes
consume over time. To monitor usage, see
[Usage](/manuals/billing/_index.md#usage).

Docker Agentic Platform requires that you bring your own API keys
for inference. Your inference provider handles those costs.

> [!NOTE]
> Docker Agentic Platform plans are a Docker Personal and Docker Pro
> exclusive.

## Billing behaviors

Docker Agentic Platform is billed monthly on the day you subscribe. Your invoice reflects Docker Sandbox compute usage accrued during the previous period. For example:

- If you subscribed on September 10, then you are billed on October 10.
- Your invoice from October 10 reflects accrued usage from September 10 through October 9.

To track usage for Docker Agentic Platform:

1. Sign in to [Docker Home](https://app.docker.com/), then choose your organization account.
1. Go to **Billing** to view the Overview page, then go to **Active plans**.
1. Select **Manage** next to **Docker Agentic Platform**.
1. View the estimated totals with usage for the current pay period.

## Cancel a plan

Canceling Docker Agentic Platform disables plan renewal at the
start of the next month. Docker bills you for usage at the end of the plan
period.

1. Sign in to [Docker Home](https://app.docker.com/) and go to **Billing**.
1. From **Active plans**, select **Manage** next to
   **Docker Agentic Platform**.
1. Select **Cancel subscription**.
1. Review usage behavior, then select **Cancel subscription** to confirm.
