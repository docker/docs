Gordon is free to use and comes with a usage quota. In Docker Desktop and the
CLI, usage counts toward your Docker account's quota.

## Usage limits

The number of questions you can ask depends on their complexity. Questions
that involve more context, files, or Docker operations use more of your quota.

## Reset windows

Usage limits apply across 4-hour, daily, and monthly windows. These windows
reset at fixed UTC times:

- Every four hours, starting at 00:00 UTC
- Daily at 00:00 UTC
- Monthly on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC

If you reach a usage limit, wait for the applicable window to reset before
using Gordon again.

## View your usage

Docker Desktop shows a usage indicator so you can see how close you are to
your limit.
