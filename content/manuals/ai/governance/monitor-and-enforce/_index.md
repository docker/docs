After policies are configured, use monitoring, audit records, and sign-in
enforcement to verify how governance behaves across developer machines.

- [Monitoring policies](/manuals/ai/governance/monitor-and-enforce/monitoring.md): inspect active policy rules and monitor
  sandbox network traffic.
- [Audit logs](/manuals/ai/governance/audit/_index.md): view, configure, export, and collect governance audit
  records.
- [Sign-in enforcement](/manuals/ai/governance/monitor-and-enforce/sign-in-enforcement.md): require users to sign in as
  members of approved Docker organizations.
